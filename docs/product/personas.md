# Personas

## P1 — Ramesh Patel, 45 · Hoshangabad, MP · 3 acres · wheat (Rabi) + soybean (Kharif)
Hindi-only. Android phone (₹8k), WhatsApp-level digital literacy. Patchy 4G. Decisions by tradition + neighbors. Pain: irrigates on habit (diesel pump cost), sold soybean early last year and lost ₹9k vs waiting two weeks.
**Needs:** verdicts not data; Hindi; big buttons; camera-first disease check; voice output helpful.
**Design consequences:** default-Hindi flow, ≤2 taps to today-verdict, icon+color+text triple coding, TTS "read aloud" on recommendations, cached reads for patchy network.

## P2 — Priya Deshmukh, 28 · Nashik, MH · 6 acres · tomato + onion
Bilingual, smartphone-fluent, sells at Pimpalgaon mandi. Pain: tomato disease spreads before she recognizes it; onion price whiplash; wants reasons before trusting an app.
**Needs:** early disease flags with confidence, price trends + mandi comparison, explanation traces, history.
**Design consequences:** why-trace UI, trend charts with plain-language signal, health log timeline, community outbreak visibility.

## P3 — Anil Kumar, 35 · Extension-adjacent progressive farmer, Varanasi, UP · rice + potato
Tries new tools, becomes village reference point. Would use crop recommendation + fertilizer planning; shares alerts onward.
**Design consequences:** shareable/readable advisories, crop recommendation wizard, soil-test-aware fertilizer guidance.

## Anti-persona guardrail
We are NOT building for agri-corporates, traders, or researchers. Any feature serving them and not P1–P3 is out of scope.
