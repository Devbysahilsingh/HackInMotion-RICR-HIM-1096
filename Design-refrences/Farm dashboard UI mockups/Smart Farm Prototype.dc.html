Scanned 13 Aug, 9:38 am · Onion, bulb development, 15 acre

What we saw
  • Small sunken spots with pale centres
  • Purple-brown rings spreading outward
  • Damage concentrated on older leaves
  • Leaf tips drying back
What to do now
1

Remove affected leaves

Cut and carry them out of the field. Do not leave them between the rows.

2

Water at the base, in the morning

Overhead watering and overnight leaf wetness are what spread this.

3

Give the rows air

Clear weeds between rows so leaves dry faster after rain.

What to watch over the next week
Spots reaching the neck of the bulbAct fast
New spots on younger, upper leavesSpreading
No new spots after three dry daysHolding
When to get help

If more than a quarter of the field shows spots, or the bulbs soften, speak to your KVK or call the Kisan Call Centre on 1800-180-1551. We do not issue pesticide names or doses.

Fertiliser plan

Based on TNAU / ICAR published doses
Done · at sowing

Basal dose

20:60:20

N:P:K kg per hectare, applied 28 June. Phosphorus matters most for soybean root nodulation.

Next · within 10 days

Sulphur, at pod set

20 kg/ha

Soybean is a heavy sulphur user and Malwa black soils are commonly short of it. Apply to moist soil — the forecast rain is a good window.

Later · not yet due

No top dressing

Soybean fixes its own nitrogen once nodules form. Adding urea now suppresses that and wastes money.

Why this plan
Published basal dose (state package of practices)20:60:20 Your area20 acre · 8.1 ha Soil test on fileNone Sulphur status assumedDeficient (regional default)

Without a soil test this is a regional package, not a plan for your field. A soil health card would replace the assumed values with measured ones — your nearest testing centre is at Karond.

What we do not tell you

No brand, no pesticide, no chemical dose for a disease. Nutrient doses come from published state packages of practices; anything beyond that belongs to your KVK officer, who can see the field.
